# Demonstration model provenance

Both GLB files were created specifically for this prototype by scripts/generate-models.mjs. They contain original procedural geometry and flat materials, with no external meshes, images, fonts, or textures. These demonstration assets may be reused and modified under CC0-1.0.

They do not depict any real building. The model labeled 1800 is an invented visual comparison, not an accurate reconstruction. Both models share a coordinate system: +Y up, +Z entrance, origin at ground center, nominal units in meters. The current version has a taller copper roof and lantern; the historical version has a low terracotta roof and chimney.
